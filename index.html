FAQ

Common questions answered.

INSURANCE & SELF-PAY

I accept Anthem and UnitedHealthcare. I also offer self-pay options with a sliding scale fee structure to help make therapy accessible. During our consultation, we can discuss rates and coverage that work for your situation.

SUPPORT FOR EVERYONE

If you're feeling overwhelmed, stuck, or just want to optimize your life, therapy is a great tool. You don't need to be in crisis to benefit from a strategy session.

LOCATION

I'm based in Cincinnati and licensed to practice throughout the state of Ohio. Sessions are available virtually, so you can connect from anywhere in the state.

SESSION LENGTH

Standard sessions are 50 minutes long. This gives us enough time to tackle current issues and set up tools for the week ahead.
