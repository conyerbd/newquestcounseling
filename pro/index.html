Common questions

Do you take insurance?

I accept Anthem and UnitedHealthcare. I also offer self-pay with a sliding scale to help make therapy accessible. On the free consultation we can go over rates and coverage that work for your situation.

Is therapy right for me?

If you're feeling overwhelmed or stuck, or you just want a clearer plan, therapy can help. You don't need to be in crisis to benefit.

Where are you located?

I'm based in Cincinnati and licensed throughout Ohio. Sessions are by video, so you can connect from anywhere in the state, whether that's Cincinnati, Columbus, Cleveland, or a small town in between.

How long are sessions?

Standard sessions are 50 minutes. That gives us time to work on what's going on now and set up tools for the week ahead.

Do you work with teens?

Yes, ages 14 and up, with one-on-one sessions, family sessions, and parent check-ins. More for parents
